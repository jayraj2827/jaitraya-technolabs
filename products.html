Mulyankan AI

Live Platform Beta
Education Technology • Computer Vision & Applied AI • Higher Education
Platform Overview mulyankanai.in ↗

An AI-powered academic evaluation platform for handwritten descriptive examination answer sheets. Built with a teacher-in-the-loop philosophy, Mulyankan AI automates answer-sheet ingestion, unconstrained OCR handwriting extraction, question segmentation, and rubric-based assessment while ensuring educators retain ultimate grading authority.

• Answer-sheet upload & processing
• Vision OCR handwriting extraction
• Question-wise answer segmentation
• Rubric-based contextual evaluation
• Balanced, lenient & strict modes
• Professor moderation & manual overrides
• Structured evaluation reports
• Excel-based institutional metrics
Institutional Focus: Higher Education Universities • Autonomous Colleges • Examination Boards

MIRA

Research Lab Preview 100% Air-Gapped
Offline Multimodal Knowledge Retrieval • Vector RAG • Scientific Research Institutes
Platform Architecture Request Lab Pilot →

An offline multimodal information retrieval workstation engineered specifically for scientific research institutes and academic laboratories. Operating entirely air-gapped without cloud dependencies, MIRA ingests research papers, diagram schematics, and symposium audio recordings — enabling context-aware semantic search with local LLM synthesis and exact citations.

• 100% Air-gapped offline sovereignty
• Tri-modal search (Text, Diagrams, Audio)
• Local ChromaDB & FAISS vector storage
• Local LLM execution via Ollama (Llama-3/Mistral)
• Page number & audio timestamp citations
• Cross-platform Electron.js desktop client
• High-performance Python FastAPI service
• Local SQLite metadata & state persistence
Institutional Focus: Scientific Research Institutes • University R&D Labs • Air-Gapped Archives
