Mulyankan AI

Intelligent Handwritten Descriptive Assessment with Teacher-in-the-Loop

Mulyankan AI is an academic evaluation platform built for higher education institutions, universities, coaching academies, and examination boards. It automates answer-sheet OCR, question segmentation, and rubric-based assessment while placing final grading authority firmly in the hands of educators.

Institutional Problem

The Bottleneck of Manual Examination Grading

Evaluating thousands of physical handwritten answer booklets is one of the most resource-intensive operational challenges in academia.

Grader Fatigue

Faculty spend hundreds of hours manually evaluating physical paper scripts, leading to cognitive fatigue, inconsistent marking over time, and weeks of reporting delay.

Subjective Variance

Different examiners apply inconsistent standards across the same subject, making standardized institutional moderation difficult and driving high revaluation volumes.

Physical Logistics

Physical transport, storage, and ledger entry of paper answer sheets introduce significant handling overhead, security concerns, and administration costs.

Workflow Architecture

The 4-Stage Evaluation Process

How examination scripts move through Mulyankan AI from ingestion to certified grade release.

Stage 01

Exam Blueprint Setup

Upload the digital question paper, configure course codes, define maximum marks per question, and establish the master scoring rubric or model answers.

Stage 02

Vision OCR & Segmentation

Scanned answer booklets undergo automatic deskewing, noise reduction, and handwriting extraction with intelligent question-wise answer segmentation.

Stage 03

Rubric & Step Scoring

Student responses are evaluated against marking criteria step-by-step under selectable rigor profiles: Balanced, Lenient, or Strict evaluation modes.

Stage 04

Faculty Moderation

Professors review evaluated scripts on an interactive digital canvas with 1-click approvals, manual score overrides, and instant Excel export.
