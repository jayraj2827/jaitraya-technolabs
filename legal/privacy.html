Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 2026

1. Institutional Commitment to Privacy

Jaitraya Technolabs ("Company", "we", "our") engineers technology products that process institutional and educational data. We adhere strictly to data minimization, cryptographic isolation, and zero unauthorized third-party sharing.

2. Examination & Student Data Protection

When universities and examination authorities deploy platforms such as Mulyankan AI:

  • • Student identity numbers and roll codes are isolated and never combined with public training corpora.
  • • Scanned answer booklets are processed within secure, encrypted multi-tenant partitions.
  • • Client institutional data is never sold, leased, or monetized for commercial advertising.

3. Web Inquiries & Correspondence

Information transmitted through our web contact forms (name, institutional email, phone, organization) is used exclusively to respond to your specific inquiry, execute pilot demonstrations, or evaluate employment applications.

4. Privacy Officer Contact

For data protection questions, audit verification requests, or institutional compliance agreements, please contact our Data Governance Desk at: privacy@jaitraya.com.
