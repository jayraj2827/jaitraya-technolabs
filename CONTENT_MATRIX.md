### Page 4: Mulyankan AI Dedicated Page (`/products/mulyankan-ai`)
* **URL:** `/products/mulyankan-ai`
* **Title:** Mulyankan AI | Intelligent Descriptive Assessment Platform
* **Meta Description:** Explore Mulyankan AI, an AI-powered academic evaluation system for handwritten descriptive exams featuring teacher-in-the-loop moderation and rubric-based scoring.
* **Sections:**
  1. **Product Hero:** Independent brand color accents (deep indigo / electric blue), verified product badge, headline, platform preview CTA, and direct link to `mulyankanai.in`.
  2. **The Assessment Bottleneck (The Problem):**
     - Tens of thousands of handwritten scripts evaluated manually per exam cycle.
     - Severe grader fatigue, inconsistent scoring criteria, and weeks of reporting delay.
     - Traditional OCR fails on unstructured cursive handwriting and multi-step mathematical/diagrammatic answers.
  3. **The Mulyankan Solution:**
     - End-to-end evaluation pipeline that converts physical answer booklets into verifiable digital assessments.
     - Faculty remains in complete control ("Teacher-in-the-Loop").
  4. **10 Verified Core Capabilities (Detailed Breakdown):**
     1. Answer-sheet upload and high-resolution batch digitization.
     2. Document preprocessing, skew correction, and noise reduction.
     3. Question-answer segmentation and bounding-box mapping.
     4. Rubric-based contextual evaluation against standard grading criteria.
     5. Answer-key-based factual and step-by-step scoring.
     6. Automated rubric generation from sample model answers.
     7. Multi-tier evaluation modes: *Balanced*, *Lenient*, and *Strict*.
     8. Professor moderation interface with 1-click approvals and manual overrides.
     9. Structured student evaluation reports with annotated feedback.
     10. Excel-compatible assessment metrics and institutional grade distributions.
  5. **4-Stage Workflow Pipeline (Visual Step-by-Step):**
     - Step 1: Question Paper & Blueprint Configuration.
     - Step 2: Ingestion & Vision-based Script Segmentation.
     - Step 3: Rubric Matching & AI Step Scoring.
     - Step 4: Faculty Moderation & Institutional Grade Release.
  6. **Institutional Target Applications:**
     - State & Central Universities
     - Autonomous Engineering & Medical Colleges
     - National & State Examination Boards
     - High-Volume Competitive Exam Coaching Academies
  7. **Call to Action:** `Request Institutional Pilot` / `Visit Mulyankan AI Official Site`.
